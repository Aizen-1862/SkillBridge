# SkillBridge Product Specification

**Learn. Build. Collaborate. Grow.** A student-first project and collaboration platform for college students across India.

## Audiences
- Students: discover projects, find teammates and build portfolios.
- Student creators: publish ideas and recruit collaborators.
- Startups: publish scoped projects and discover student talent.
- Administrators: review verification requests and reports.

## MVP
- Email/password accounts and student profiles (college, course, branch, year, bio, skills).
- College directory and student verification request status.
- Student- and startup-created project publishing and discovery.
- Search/filter by skill, level, type and status.
- Applications with owner review; teams and milestones.
- Portfolio entries tied to projects and contributions.
- Notifications, report/block and moderation workflows before public launch.

## Verification
Use confirmed college email domains where possible; otherwise private student-ID review or manual institution review. Keep documents in private storage with restricted access and a deletion schedule. Public profiles show status only, never ID documents. Startup publishers require separate organization review.

## Trust and safety
Private contact details by default. No student application fees. Disclose compensation and expected workload. Add age-appropriate safeguards for under-18 users, including limits on direct contact and opportunity eligibility. Implement reporting, blocking, moderation, audit logs and account export/deletion before launch.

## Roadmap
1. Foundation and onboarding  2. Verification  3. Projects and applications  4. Teams and milestones  5. Portfolios  6. Opportunities  7. Recommendations  8. Security and pilot testing.
